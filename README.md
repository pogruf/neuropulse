# Privacy Policy for NeuroPulse

**Last updated: September 08, 2026**

This privacy policy governs your use of the mobile application **NeuroPulse: Biorhythms & CNS** (hereinafter referred to as the "Application") created by **pogruf** (hereinafter referred to as the "Developer").

The Application is designed as a high-tech, offline-first personal biohacking and central nervous system (CNS) monitoring tool. It allows users to track biological waves, critical days, and team synchronization based on mathematical algorithms.

---

## 1. Absolute Privacy & Offline Enforcement (Zero-Knowledge)

The Application is built on a strict **Zero-Knowledge Architecture**. 
* **No Server Infrastructure:** The Application operates 100% autonomously on your mobile device. The Developer does not maintain any external servers, databases, cloud infrastructure, or external APIs.
* **No Internet Access:** The Application does not require, request, or utilize an internet connection or network data transfer to perform its core functions.
* **No Tracking or Analytics:** The Application does not contain any third-party SDKs, tracking tools, advertisement identifiers, or user analytics systems.

---

## 2. Information Collection and Use

### Personal Data & Sensitive Information
To perform mathematical calculations of biorhythm waves, the Application requires the input of the following local parameters:
* **Name / Profile Identifier** (for profile sorting)
* **Exact Date and Time of Birth** (required for chronological age calculation in days)
* **Biological Gender** (required for mathematical amplitude tuning of the intellectual wave metrics)
* **Daily Well-being Scores** (manually provided by the user for the built-in Least Squares Method calibration engine)

### Storage of Local Data
All provided data remains strictly under your control. It is written and kept locally on your mobile device inside an encrypted standalone SQLite database. The data never leaves your device and is inaccessible to the Developer or any third-party entities.

---

## 3. Data Deletion and Retention

Since all information is stored exclusively on your local device, the Developer does not retain or manage your data. 
* You can modify, overwrite, or delete individual profile records or calibration metrics directly through the Application UI.
* **Complete Erasure:** Uninstalling the Application from your device or clearing the Application data via the system settings will completely and permanently delete the local database along with all profiles and history.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy

The Application does not collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13, as no data is transmitted externally. The Application does not perform biometric age checks and relies entirely on user privacy isolation within the secure offline device sandboxing.

The Developer may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be made available by posting the new Privacy Policy document directly within this repository. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
